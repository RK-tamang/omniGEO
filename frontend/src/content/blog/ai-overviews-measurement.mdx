An AI Overview can rank you first and still send you less traffic than the link below it. That sentence breaks most SEO dashboards, which assume position one equals the biggest share of clicks.

The fix is not to throw out rank tracking. It is to add the layer above it.

## What actually changed

Three things, in order of importance:

1. **The answer occupies the space before the link.** Even when you are cited, the user may get what they need without clicking.
2. **The click, when it comes, is later and more qualified.** Buyers arrive having already read a synthesized comparison.
3. **The set of sources is smaller.** An Overview typically cites a handful of pages, which means visibility is less evenly distributed than a ten-link SERP.

None of these mean SEO is dead. They mean “position” is now one metric among several.

## The metrics that still matter

### Impressions and clicks, but separated

If you connect Google Search Console, break performance down by query type and by whether an AI Overview appeared. A flat click curve with rising impressions is a signal that the Overview is absorbing demand — not that your content got worse.

### AI citation share

For your tracked prompt set, what percentage of answers cite your domain? This is the most direct measure of the new layer. Track it per engine, because citations in AI Overviews and citations in ChatGPT do not move together.

### Branded search volume

This is the lagging indicator that most teams ignore. Being named in answers creates familiarity, and familiarity creates branded searches. If AI mentions rise and branded search follows six to eight weeks later, you are winning even if clicks are flat.

### Assisted conversions

Segment analytics by whether the session touched a page cited in an AI answer, or came from a branded search after an answer impression. Attribution will be imperfect — it always is — but directional evidence beats a single conversion number.

## A dashboard that answers one question

The question is: *are we becoming the answer?*

Four panels:

| Panel | Metric | Cadence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Presence | Mention rate by engine | Weekly |
| Authority | Citation share and cited domains | Weekly |
| Demand | Branded search volume | Monthly |
| Outcome | Assisted pipeline from AI-touched sessions | Monthly |

If presence and authority rise but demand does not, the answers may be mentioning you in low-intent questions. If demand rises but pipeline does not, the promise in the answer is not matching the landing page.

## What to do when an Overview cites competitors instead of you

Work backwards from the citation. The answer usually cites two or three sources: a listicle, a review site, and a competitor page. Ask three questions:

- Is our equivalent page retrievable and unambiguous?
- Do third-party sources in this category mention us at all?
- Does our page contain the specific claim the Overview needs?

Then fix the weakest link first. Often it is the third one: the page exists but never states the comparison in a sentence a model can lift.

## Reporting it without panic

Stakeholders see traffic down and assume failure. Show them the full picture: impressions, citation share, branded search and pipeline. Put the AI layer next to the classic layer, on the same page, with the same time axis.
